# ho-07.6 — The emergence, alive: settlements build, beacons light, the writing speaks in its own voice

ho-07.2 landed the world-then-writing emergence: peaks rise, a renamed pair pulsed,
towns faded in, the world held before the writing. Watching it run, the practitioner
steered (forward-only — this ho responds, ho-07.2 stays as built):

- The towns should not arrive fully-formed. They should **build house by house** —
  the settlement growing through hamlet → village → town → city as its houses
  accumulate, the **cathedral built last**. The romance is watching the civilization
  construct itself.
- The rise reads right as a **fade** (the cross-fade). The one thing that *pulsed* —
  the terracotta rename rings — read as "weird red rings." **Retire the rings**; the
  rename becomes a **soft re-glow** (a fade, in the language the rest of the animation
  already speaks).
- The peak names should **fade in, offset** — each name appearing after its peak
  rises, staggered, not all-at-once.
- **Peak text and town text must be differentiated.** Grounded in the brand system
  (consulted this session): towns are *authorial voice* → **italic, mixed-case, muted
  ink**; peaks stay **upright wide-tracked caps in ink** + native script. Differentiated
  by voice and weight, **not** color (see Decision 4 — terracotta is reserved).
- A **subtle glowing beacon** on each peak — the signal-fires-of-Gondor read — lit as
  the peak rises.
- The **corpus-floor marker** was invisible (parked at the very bottom edge, 0.5px) —
  the practitioner couldn't find it. Make it legible enough to judge, or cut it.
- The **world→writing hold** was invisible (a pause has no picture). With the towns now
  visibly *building*, the second act announces itself — reduce the hold to a breath.

**Out of scope:**
- **The beat timeline and `emergenceScale` hook** (ho-07.2) — the world rise, the
  partition, the plan, the field math all stand. This ho changes *rendering and the
  town sub-animation*, not the timeline.
- **Relationship terrain (ho-08)** — roads, ridges, trails.
- **Click interaction and cards (ho-09)** — the beacons and the type are static-render
  flourishes; interaction (and terracotta's clickable role) is ho-09.
- **Mobile cartography** — desktop-only path unchanged.
- **Data / schema / Indexer** — no changes.

## Phase 1 — Think (resolved live with the practitioner, 2026-06-14)

### Decision 1 — House-by-house settlement growth: reveal blocks incrementally as a town arrives, cathedral last.

A town's blocks (`growSettlement`) are revealed over its arrival, not all at once. The
player drives a per-town sub-timeline: houses appear one (or a few) at a time on a quick
inner beat, the settlement growing in place; the single `terra` cathedral block (a city's
landmark) is held back and revealed **last**. Because a town's final size band is fixed
(from its `settlementWeight`), a city under construction *passes through* hamlet → village
→ town silhouettes as its houses accumulate — one mechanism delivers "hamlets turn into
cities." Pure render: settlements are SVG blocks (no heightfield, no re-contour), so this
is cheap regardless of cadence. The reveal order and per-house timing are by-feel tuners.

**Architecture:** a pure helper in `settlements.js` (or `emergence.js`) that, given a
town's blocks and a reveal fraction `0→1`, returns the visible prefix with the cathedral
gated to the final fraction — test-first. The player walks each arriving town's fraction
on a sub-beat while the existing town fade-alpha layers on top.

### Decision 2 — The rename becomes a soft re-glow; the rings are retired.

`pulseSvg` (the terracotta rings) is removed. On a `pulse` step, the renamed peaks
(forteller / pālana) instead **re-glow**: a brief brightening of the peak — reuse the
beacon glow (Decision 5) at elevated intensity, easing back to rest — rendered through the
same fade language, no ring, no terracotta. The pulse *beat* in the timeline is unchanged
(ho-07.2's `emergence.js` is untouched); only its rendering changes.

### Decision 3 — Peak names fade in, offset from the rise and staggered within a beat.

Peak labels animate in via opacity (the `emgIn` language), **delayed** after their peak's
contour rise lands, and **staggered** when a beat carries several same-day peaks (a small
per-label offset). "Appear, then fade in" — the terrain rises, then its name settles. The
delay and stagger step are by-feel tuners. Implemented as CSS `animation-delay` per label
in the emerging layer.

### Decision 4 — Peak vs town typography: differentiated by *voice*, not color (brand-grounded).

Consulted the ATM brand system this session. Two load-bearing findings:
- **Terracotta is the interaction color** ("if it's terracotta, it's clickable") and this
  project's frozen register spends it **once**, on the city's cathedral landmark. So town
  labels in terracotta would miscode the map and pre-spend ho-09's interaction signal.
  **Rejected** the practitioner's tentative "maybe towns in terracotta."
- The brand reserves **italic for authorial voice.** Towns *are* authorial voice (writing
  about the work). So: **peaks** = upright, wide-tracked **caps, ink** (`#2B2B2B`) + native
  script (unchanged); **towns** = **italic, mixed-case, muted ink** (`#6B6B6B`/brand Ink
  Muted), smaller, below the settlement. Differentiation by register, no color conflict.

### Decision 5 — The beacon is amber/flame, not terracotta; a register evolution flagged for approval.

A subtle warm glow on each peak summit, lit as the peak's rise completes — the
signal-fires read. Color is **amber `#d4952a`** (brand "highlights") / ochre, **not**
terracotta — a signal fire is flame-colored, and keeping it off terracotta preserves that
color's reserved meaning (landmark + future interaction). This adds **one warm accent** to
the frozen 3-color cartographic register (ink / paper / terracotta); recorded here as a
**register evolution** for the practitioner's explicit approval. The beacon is a soft
radial glow (low opacity, a small warm core), steady — not a harsh pulse. Whether it lights
in LOTR sequence (each beacon at its peak's rise) or all hold steady is by-feel.

### Decision 6 — The corpus-floor marker: make it legible or cut it; the practitioner judges once it's visible.

It was invisible (y≈610, the bottom edge, 0.5px). Reposition it to a real, readable horizon
(or render it as a clearer quiet rule with a legible caption) so the practitioner can
finally see it and make a real keep/cut call — the by-feel landing decides. Cutting is a
one-line removal if it still doesn't earn its place.

### Decision 7 — The hold shrinks to a breath.

The world→writing hold is reduced to a short settle (the visible town-building now carries
the act change). Kept as a tuner so it can go to zero by feel.

### Decision 8 — Timing/feel are by-feel tuners, seeded watchable, landed live.

Following the ho-07.2 / ho-07.5 posture: house reveal interval, cathedral-last timing, name
fade delay + stagger, beacon size/opacity/intensity, corpus-floor weight, hold breath — all
wired into the existing tuner panel, seeded sane, dialed against the register.

## Phase 2 — Execute

One inhabited session, watched (feel-heavy). Build order:

1. **House-reveal helper** in `settlements.js` — pure, test-first: given blocks + a reveal
   fraction, return the visible prefix with the cathedral gated last. (Decision 1)
2. **Town sub-animation** in the player — each arriving town walks its reveal fraction on a
   sub-beat under the existing fade-alpha. (Decision 1)
3. **Typography differentiation** — towns to italic/mixed-case/muted; peaks unchanged.
   (Decision 4)
4. **Beacon glow** — amber radial per peak, lit on rise. (Decision 5)
5. **Peak-name offset fade-in** — per-label `animation-delay` + stagger. (Decision 3)
6. **Rename re-glow** — retire `pulseSvg`; pulse step re-glows the renamed peaks. (Decision 2)
7. **Corpus-floor legibility** (Decision 6) and **hold breath** (Decision 7).
8. **Tuners** — wire the new feel parameters; land by feel. (Decision 8)

### Testing
The pure helper (Decision 1) is unit-tested to the 90% floor from fixtures. Everything else
is render/CSS/feel in the coverage-excluded `cartography-page.js`, dialed live. Verification
rhythm each step: lint, typecheck, test (≥90%), commit `ho-07.6:`.

### Done means
- Towns build house-by-house, cathedrals last; a city visibly grows through smaller forms.
- Peak names fade in offset/staggered; peak vs town text reads as two distinct voices.
- Each peak carries a subtle amber beacon, lit as it rises; no terracotta on non-landmark
  elements.
- The rename is a soft re-glow; the red rings are gone.
- The corpus-floor marker is legible-or-cut by feel; the hold is a breath.
- lint / typecheck / test green; commits atomic and `ho-07.6:`-prefixed.
