# ho-06.5 — Tuner landing: the by-feel pass, in the practitioner's hand

## Problem

ho-06 closed declaring the field tuners held — every default unchanged, confirmed against the contour register. That verdict was reached on the implementing model's eye, before the practitioner ran the by-feel pass himself on the live instrument. The by-feel pass is the practitioner's call against the register, not the model's; run in the practitioner's hand across several seeds and filter states, it landed nowhere near "unchanged." ho-06 is closed and stays closed with its premature verdict as the record (forward-only); this ho supersedes the tuner landing.

Two instrument gaps surfaced during the pass and were closed live on the debug page: the panel exposed `radius × importance` but not the base radius, so the importance-spread lever wasn't reachable — a `base radius` slider was added. And the sink floor's right value turned out to interact with the base radius: dropping the base makes low-importance non-matching works collapse to dots under a filter, so the floor that reads as "small hills" had to rise.

## Solution

Lock the practitioner's dialed values as the new defaults across the three files that own them. The map he blessed: a dense field of individuated hills (low base radius, tight ring spacing, low crenellation), importance reading as prominence (`radius × importance` up relative to the base), softer summits, and non-matching works receding under a filter to legible small hills rather than naked dots (floor up).

| tuner | ho-06 default | locked | owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| ring spacing (`interval`) | 0.62 | **0.32** | `contours.js` `CONTOUR_DEFAULTS` |
| crenellation (`noiseWeight`) | 0.85 | **0.3** | `field.js` `DEFAULTS` |
| summit sharpness (`summitExp`) | 1.7 | **1.15** | `field.js` `DEFAULTS` |
| base radius (`radiusBase`) | 40 | **12** | `field.js` `DEFAULTS` |
| radius × importance (`radiusScale`) | 16 | **8** | `field.js` `DEFAULTS` |
| sink floor (`relevanceFloor`) | 0.15 | **0.45** | `cartographer.js` |

`cell` (4), `candidates` (12), `indexEvery` (5), and the register stroke weights (0.25 / 0.7) are unchanged — geometry and register, not feel.

Two diagnostics from the pass, recorded but **not** classification changes:

- **"Some peaks aren't really peaks" was the filter working, not a misclassification.** Pink Teaming (importance 7) reads as a flat dot under `continuity + craft + infrastructure` because its themes are `relationship, attention` — it doesn't match, so it sinks. Unfiltered, it stands tall. Decision 5 (peak vs town, by `group`) holds; this is Decision 2 (one terrain, non-matches sink) doing its job.
- **The floor interacts with the base radius.** At base radius 40 a sunk work kept a wide radius, so even at 0.3× amplitude it spread a readable low mound. At base radius 12 a sunk work has a small radius *and* low amplitude, so it collapses to a dot — the base-radius drop made the sink more aggressive, which is why the floor rose from 0.15 to 0.45 to keep non-matching works legible.

## Changes

- **`src/field.js`** `DEFAULTS`: `summitExp` 1.7→1.15, `noiseWeight` 0.85→0.3, `radiusBase` 40→12, `radiusScale` 16→8.
- **`src/contours.js`** `CONTOUR_DEFAULTS`: `interval` 0.62→0.32.
- **`src/cartographer.js`** `DEFAULT_RELEVANCE_FLOOR`: 0.15→0.45.
- **`src/cartography-page.js`**: the panel opens on the new defaults; the `base radius` slider added during the pass.
- **Tests**: the two contour fixtures that hardcoded the old `interval` (0.62) now pin it explicitly, so they assert geometry, not the default value.

## Results

- Full stack green: `npm run lint`, `npm test` (≥90% on the cartography modules), `npm run typecheck`.
- The debug page opens on the locked defaults; `cartography.html` renders the practitioner's blessed register on load.
- Supersedes ho-06's tuner verdict (forward-only): ho-06 records "held by the model's eye," ho-06.5 records "landed by the practitioner's eye."
- The overview's ho-05/06 field-tuner notes can refresh downstream (Kamae-4 territory; flagged, not edited).

**Followup:** ho-07 (towns) builds on this field at the locked values — settlements sit on the terrain these defaults shape.
