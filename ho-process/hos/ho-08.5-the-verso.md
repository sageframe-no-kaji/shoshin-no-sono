# ho-08.5 — The verso: the back of the map

Every serious map has a back. This ho builds it: the full-text reverse side of the cartography — the gazetteer, the legend, the survey notes — reached by turning the map over. The verso subsumes the ho-03 grid view; ho-03 stays closed (forward-only), and this document records the supersession.

The ho resolves the two tensions the map face carries deliberately. First: the map is an argument ("the work is one project") that resists indexing — a visitor who wants to *find* something has to decode a private semiotic system. The verso is where the routing lives: the argument on the front, the index on the back, one flip apart. Second: the map face explains none of its own marks. The legend belongs on the back of a map, which is where legends have always belonged — the face keeps its reticence, the verso resolves it.

The verso is also the surface machines and skimmers actually read. The map face is SVG labels — mediocre for crawlers, opaque to screen readers, skipped by anyone in a hurry. The verso is semantic HTML: headings, lists, links. Search engines, screen readers, reduced-motion visitors, and people who just want the list all enter here.

**Out of scope.** The flip gesture's interaction polish and animation — ho-09 owns interaction; this ho ships a plain, working affordance. Mobile layout of the verso (ho-13, which now points its tap-through fallback here instead of at the grid). SEO metadata beyond semantic markup (ho-14). Any change to the map face's renderers, the emergence player, or the ho-08 feature vocabulary.

**Resolves deferred decisions** (from the ho-overview's ho-08.5 entry): the verso's name and door; grid retirement mechanics; verso composition at v1.

**Blocked on:** ho-08 closing. The legend explains the map face's full mark vocabulary — trails, roads, water, whatever ho-08's by-feel landing keeps — so this ho's execution starts after ho-08's Reflect is written. Authored now so the ho-08 close and ho-09 authoring both know the verso is next.

## Phase 1 — Think

The Think phase happened in the design conversation of 2026-07-10/11 (the repo-review session that also named the two tensions). Decisions recorded here with their rationale; three were listed as open in the K4 entry and are resolved below with the conversation's reasoning.

### Decision 1 — The door: `?view=back`; the visitor-facing name is "the back of the map."

The Gate gains a `view` URL key — the key its own grammar comment anticipated ("future keys (focus, view) degrade gracefully"). `parseView(search)` returns `'back'` for `?view=back` and `'map'` for absent, empty, or unrecognized values — unknown values degrade to the map face, consistent with the Gate's ignore-what-you-don't-know philosophy. Default state emits no `view` key; `?view=back` is the only serialized form, so shareable URLs stay clean. Filter state and seed carry across the flip in both directions: `?theme=craft&view=back` is a door to the craft gazetteer, and flipping back lands on the craft map at the same seed.

"Verso" is the working name in documents (this one, the K4 entry). The visitor-facing language is plainer: the affordance reads **"turn the map over"** on the face and **"back to the map"** on the verso. The URL word is `back` because doors read clean and `?view=verso` makes a visitor look up a word.

### Decision 2 — The verso is a pure render module: `src/verso.js`.

Same posture as every renderer in the project: `(indexer, filterState, opts) → HTML string`, no DOM, no URL, no fetch. The page owns `innerHTML` and event wiring. The module queries the Indexer for everything — works, groups, group intros, theme vocabulary — and never parses raw catalog JSON (the CLAUDE.md boundary). Register colors come from `src/register.js`. Tested like the other renderers: string assertions, edge cases, the 90% floor.

### Decision 3 — Composition at v1: legend, gazetteer, colophon, in that order.

Three sections, sandwich-shaped — short, long, short:

1. **The legend** explains every mark the ho-08 map face renders: elevation and what it encodes, contour and hachure as two registers of the same terrain, why writing is a town and what the sizes mean, the terracotta cathedral, trails versus roads, water, the beacon, the corpus-floor line. A couple hundred words. The legend's sample swatches are drawn by the same pure renderers that draw the map — a small contour ring from `contour-map.js`, a road fragment from `feature-map.js`, a town block from `settlement-map.js` — so the legend cannot drift from the face; it is generated, not illustrated.
2. **The gazetteer** indexes every work, ordered by work group (the existing `sort_order_within_group` discipline), dense and typographic: name, native script where it exists, the one-liner (`hero` or truncated `short_description`), and the links — deployment, repo, essay — to canonical homes. This is the routing surface; click-through is its whole job. Filter state applies: non-matching works render dimmed rather than removed, the verso's analog of the map face's sink-not-vanish rule — one catalog, both sides.
3. **The colophon** is the survey note: composed from the theme vocabulary and group intros already in `works.json`, plus a short authored paragraph — surveyed 2025–2026, N works, drawn fresh each visit, `seed N` reproduces this plate — and the emergence's one sentence of explanation, which the animation itself deliberately withholds.

The relationship listing in prose (the distance-table analog) is **deferred** — decided by feel once the core three read right, not committed at v1.

### Decision 4 — Grid retirement: the renderer dies, the shared helpers move to their consumers.

`src/grid.js`'s catalog renderer (`renderCatalog`, `cardHtml`, `chipRow`, `filterWorks`) and `src/main.js`'s grid-page wiring retire with `index.html`'s grid markup. Two helpers survive because they were never grid-specific: `escapeHtml` moves to `src/verso.js` (its consumer), and `chipVocabulary`'s job moves into the Indexer as a derived query — `vocabulary()` — which is where a corpus-derived structure belongs (the Indexer already owns every other derived structure; the cartography page re-points its chip rendering to it). Forward-only governs documents, not code: deleting the module is a normal refactor, and ho-03's document stays closed as the record of what the grid was.

`index.html` becomes the single surface — the map with its verso — and `cartography.html` retires with the grid. The site is the map, front and back; two entry pages made sense while the grid and the debug surface were different things, and they no longer are. The vitest coverage excludes (`src/main.js`) update to match.

### Decision 5 — The flip never replays the emergence.

Arriving at `?view=back` renders the verso without booting the map's animation. Flipping face→back→face lands on the static render at the same seed and filter state — the emergence plays on map-face load and reseed only, per ho-07.6's cancel-and-snap discipline. Reduced-motion visitors who land on the face already get the static map; the verso gives them the fully-text alternative one flip away.

### Discovery (deferred to execution)

- **Verso typography.** The verso is HTML+CSS, not SVG — the register carries over (Spectral, the cream, the ink) but the scale, measure, and column behavior are landed by feel on `:8788`. CSS, not tuner sliders; the tuner discipline applies in spirit (nothing hardcoded until it's been seen).
- **Legend swatch composition.** Which marks get a drawn swatch versus prose-only — settled by what reads at swatch size, checked by eyeball.
- **The dimmed-non-matching treatment** on the gazetteer — opacity value and whether dimmed entries keep live links. Default: they do (a door is a door).
- **What ho-08 actually shipped.** The legend enumerates the *closed* ho-08 vocabulary, not the planned one — written against ho-08's Reflect.

## Phase 2 — Execute

One bounded session after ho-08 closes. No agent-task decomposition — the seams (Gate key, verso module, page wiring, grid retirement) are sequential steps of one conversation, per this project's every-ho convention.

**Sequence:**

1. `src/gate.js` — `parseView(search)` + `currentView()` + `setView(view)`; `serializeURL` emits `view=back` only when non-default; filter/seed/layer state preserved across `setView`, `view` preserved across `setState`/`setLayers`.
2. `tests/gate.test.js` — round-trips, unknown-value degradation, cross-preservation both directions.
3. `src/indexer.js` — `vocabulary()` derived query (themes/media/status, sorted unique); tests.
4. `src/verso.js` — the three sections per Decision 3; swatches via the existing pure renderers; `escapeHtml` lands here; tests to the project floor.
5. Page wiring — the flip affordance on the face, "back to the map" on the verso, render branch on `currentView()`; chips re-point to `indexer.vocabulary()`.
6. Retirement — `src/grid.js`, `src/main.js`, grid tests, `cartography.html`; `index.html` becomes the single surface; vitest excludes updated.
7. Verification rhythm: validate, lint, typecheck, test — then commit on `labs`, `ho-08.5:`-prefixed, atomic per step where natural.

### Done means

- `?view=back` lands on the verso; `?theme=craft&view=back` lands on the filtered verso; flipping either direction preserves filters, seed, and layers.
- Every work in `works.json` appears in the gazetteer with working canonical-home links; non-matching works dim, never vanish.
- The legend accounts for every mark type the closed ho-08 face renders, with generated swatches where they read.
- The colophon composes from the Indexer (group intros, theme vocabulary) plus the authored survey note.
- The grid is gone: no `src/grid.js`, no `src/main.js`, no second text surface; `index.html` is the site.
- A screen reader traverses the verso as a sensible document — headings, lists, links, no SVG dependency.
- The full stack is green at the 90% floor; the emergence still plays on face load and reseed only.
