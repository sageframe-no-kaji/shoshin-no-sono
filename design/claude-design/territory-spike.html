Shoshin · Territory spike — ho-05/06 preview

One field, contours everywhere

Not isolated peaks on blank paper: a single continuous heightfield (sum of radial peak functions + elevation-attenuated noise), with iso-elevation contours extracted across the whole territory by marching squares. Peaks are local maxima; saddles and valleys carry their own contours; the lowest rings enclose the whole cluster. This is the ho-05 (heightfield) + ho-06 (contour extraction) pipeline as a design spike — it validates that the frozen register (weights 0.5 / 0.95, index every 5th) reads correctly when the map is a real field. Settlements sit on the terrain; type is variant B.
