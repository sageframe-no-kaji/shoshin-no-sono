Session 1 · Peak

What does a single peak look like?

Four drawing styles for one peak — nested, non-crossing closed contour lines on cream paper. Each is generated by the same pipeline (closed paths, stroke weight, slight wobble); only the four axes change. The inset on every frame is the same peak at 80×80 px.

Recommendation
D Index contours + hand-wobble

D reads most like a hand-surveyed peak and least like a data visualization. The organic wobble and drifting summit make it look drawn by a careful hand rather than plotted, while the heavier index contour every fifth ring is the unmistakable grammar of a real topographic survey. Its medium density keeps the rings distinct, so it survives the 80×80 px shrink where the tight, uniform A collapses into noise.

parked — varying wobble amplitude and summit drift per seed lets many peaks share one style without repeating; the index-contour interval may want to scale with peak height.
