The four sizes, grown around the streets

Solid ink footprints, no shading. Each place is grown, not laid out: a few streets radiate from the centre and curve as they go — the city adds a partial ring — and chunky blocks line them, abutting like neighbours sharing party walls but never overlapping, so the street reads as the void its two walls leave. Rotation follows each curving street; only the city carries a terracotta tower-block. Tune the proportions; both the full row and the 50% row update.
