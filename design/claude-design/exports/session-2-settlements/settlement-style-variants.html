Session 2 · Settlement

What does a settlement look like at four sizes?

Three ways to draw a settlement — hamlet, village, town, city — each generated from one primitive parameterized by size level. The rows vary three axes at once: the primitive (footprints / building forms / built-up block), how growth reads (more units / larger units / denser units), and the role of terracotta (absent / outline / fill).

HamletVillageTownCity
Recommendation
AFootprint cluster, ink only

A makes the four sizes instantly rankable. Counting footprints is preattentive, so two settlements sort by size without a moment’s thought, and a cluster of building blocks is the literal grammar of an atlas built-up area rather than a chart legend. A single footprint still reads at 12 px while the city stays a compact mass that will sit beside a peak without competing with it.
