1 · The peak

FROZEN 2026-06-12. The instrument lives in the repo and opens on these values: peak-d-explorer.html.

Practitioner reservation, parked for the assembled-register checkpoint: the index-vs-regular contour distinction doesn't fully satisfy yet ("OK for now"). Re-decide it when many peaks share a page — the levers are indexRatio, the absolute weight pair, or revisiting the single-ink decision (a lighter regular gray was the pre-explorer look).

The reference model (read from the Explorer source — this is what ho-05/06 reimplement): the silhouette is a shared low-order harmonic series (k = 2–5, seeded), scaled by baseForm plus slight ellipticity — every ring nests as a parallel of it. Terrain is a summit offset, not noise: each ring's center shifts progressively along a fixed bearing (terrain × spacing × 0.72), producing the steep-face/long-shoulder asymmetry — in field terms, a skewed radial falloff per peak. amplitude is smooth per-ring value-noise (~6 control points), scaled to ring radius (clean summit) and capped at a third of the local ring gap — the no-crossing guarantee the heightfield will otherwise provide for free. Index contours count every 5th from the outermost ring. Single ink #2B2B2B; index vs regular is weight only (0.85/1.7 × lineWeight).

Register finding: the hand lives in the form, not in the line. Strokes are smooth; all character comes from silhouette (baseForm) and slope variation (terrain). Stroke noise (wobble/jitter) was explored and ruled out — the stroke renderer in ho-06 is purely geometric. Index contours every 5th ring carry the form at small scale (the 80px test).

Two further rules: crenellation decays with elevation — the tool's self-similar footprint (same lobed shape repeated to the summit) is wrong; low contours carry drainage-scale complexity, summit rings converge toward simple convex forms, so field noise attenuates with height. And the parameters split across the component boundary: baseForm/terrain are field-side (ho-05 — silhouettes fall out of the heightfield, never authored per-peak); lineWeight/ink are renderer-side (ho-06).

2 · Settlements (hamlet · village · town · city)

FROZEN 2026-06-12. The instrument lives in the repo: settlement-four-sizes.html (opens on the committed values).

Settlements are grown, not laid out. A few streets radiate from the centre and curve as they go (city adds one partial ring); chunky solid blocks line the streets, each block rotated to its street's local tangent, so the street reads as the void between two walls of buildings — no road stroke is drawn. Size is the progression: hamlet (2–3 loose blocks) → village (one informal lane) → town (2–3 corridors) → city (full corridors + ring + one terracotta tower-block in a cleared piazza). Terracotta is spent once per city and nowhere else.

Reference model (read from the explorer source — what ho-07 reimplements): buildings are placed by collision detection (oriented-rectangle SAT test with a shrink-inset), so they abut but never overlap — this is the fix for the overlap problem, and the party-wall gap is the inset. The tower is placed first so the piazza clears by collision. Each settlement is seeded; reseeding gives endless non-repeating towns in the style — which is the fresh-render-per-visit architecture, confirmed by feel. The frozen parameters tune the grammar; the seed is per-render.

Promoted to ho-07 (place-then-deform): a settlement here is grown in the abstract — terrain doesn't exist yet. At placement, the town warps to its slope (corridors bending to follow contours, footprint elongating along the valley). The peak and the settlement are revealed as the same kind of object — a seeded radial growth process with a raveling edge — so ho-07's town placement shares the growth/warp machinery with the ho-05 field. (Register's recurring rule, third occurrence: character comes from process, not styling.)

3 · Typography

FROZEN 2026-06-12 — variant B (engraved spaced caps, native beside). Instrument: map-typography.html.

One face: Spectral (Georgia fallback); native gloss on a CJK mincho stack. Hierarchy is carried by case + tracking + size, never by boxes or colour. Peak = wide-tracked roman caps (16.5px, 0.16em, all-caps) with the native script set beside at near-equal optical size (0.85×) — a co-equal name, not a footnote. Settlement = a tighter, smaller cap (11.5px, 0.22em). Elevation = a quiet italic figure. Verified: settlement caps hold at 60%, peak label holds over a contour fragment.

Coherence win: B spends no terracotta on type — the rejected variant C colored settlement names. So the only terracotta on the finished map remains the city's tower-block (session 2): one accent, one mark, one meaning. The full spec (stacks, per-class sizes/tracking/fills) is in parameters.json — consumed by ho-07 (town labels) and ho-09 (peak labels, cards).

Territory spike — the procedural reference (ho-05/06/07)

VALIDATED 2026-06-13 — territory-spike.html. The whole pipeline rendered and blessed by eye before any src/ code.

The map is one continuous heightfield, not isolated peaks: contours are extracted across the whole field by marching squares, so saddles and valleys carry their own lines and the lowest rings enclose the cluster. This file is the reference algorithm ho-05 (heightfield) and ho-06 (contour extraction) reimplement in src/ with tests. Confirmed in it: the heightfield is a sum of elliptical/rotated/harmonic radial peak functions (amplitude = importance) + 2-octave value noise weighted by (1 − summitProximity) so crenellation decays with elevation; a sharper-than-Gaussian summit profile exp(−(d/r)^1.7) avoids the flat-top; and contours render at 0.25 regular / 0.7 index, index every 5th — the map-scale weights, which resolve the session-1 index/topo reservation (the solo-peak weights were always going to be re-tuned at map scale).

Cathedral close (supersedes the session-2 city morphology): the city is a cathedral close — an elongated terracotta landmark with a long axis, a large forecourt plaza at one short end and a small apse plaza behind, both enclosed (a façade across the forecourt, a wall behind the apse), parallel flanking rows down the long sides, organic fabric beyond. Settlement density scales continuously with weight (ln(1+Σ documents-strengths)) — the four size names are bands on that continuum; the band sets the style, the weight sets the count. ho-07 owns weight→density at placement.
