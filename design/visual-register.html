1 · The peak

Candidate — freeze pending the amplitude 0 vs 1.8 A/B. Updated 2026-06-12.

Register finding: the hand lives in the form, not in the line. Strokes are smooth; all character comes from silhouette (baseForm) and slope variation (terrain). Stroke noise (wobble/jitter) was explored and ruled out — the stroke renderer in ho-06 is purely geometric. Index contours every 5th ring carry the form at small scale (the 80px test).

Two further rules: crenellation decays with elevation — the tool's self-similar footprint (same lobed shape repeated to the summit) is wrong; low contours carry drainage-scale complexity, summit rings converge toward simple convex forms, so field noise attenuates with height. And the parameters split across the component boundary: baseForm/terrain are field-side (ho-05 — silhouettes fall out of the heightfield, never authored per-peak); lineWeight/ink are renderer-side (ho-06).
